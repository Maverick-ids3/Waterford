About Waterford

Rooted in care, discipline, and discovery.

Our Identity

A school for early confidence and lifelong curiosity.

Waterford Junior & Pre-School supports children through their most formative school years. Our classrooms are designed to help learners explore ideas, discover their strengths, and learn with purpose.

The school culture values neatness, kindness, confidence, curiosity, and consistent effort. Children are guided to participate, ask questions, care for others, and take pride in their progress.

Explore

Children engage with the world through observation, questions, play, stories, experiments, and movement.

Discover

Teachers create room for learners to find new interests, talents, friendships, and ways of solving problems.

Learn

Every day builds language, numeracy, confidence, creativity, independence, and respect for others.

Our Promise

Every child should feel seen.

We keep learning personal, practical, and encouraging. Families receive clear communication, while learners receive the support and structure they need to grow at their own pace.
