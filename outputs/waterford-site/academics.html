Academics

Programs that match each stage of childhood.

01

Pre-School

Play-based routines introduce language, counting, motor skills, creativity, friendship, and classroom confidence.

  • Early literacy and phonics
  • Foundational numeracy
  • Creative play and movement
  • Social and emotional development
02

Junior School

Structured lessons help learners build knowledge, discipline, independence, and problem-solving skills.

  • English and reading fluency
  • Mathematics and mental arithmetic
  • Science and environmental awareness
  • Technology, art, sport, and life skills
03

Enrichment

Activities beyond the classroom encourage learners to lead, perform, create, and cooperate.

  • School events and assemblies
  • Clubs and creative projects
  • Sports and physical education
  • Leadership and character formation

Learning Path

Small steps, steady growth.

  1. ObserveTeachers understand each learner's strengths and needs.
  2. GuideLessons use clear routines, practice, and active participation.
  3. GrowProgress is celebrated through feedback, confidence, and responsibility.
